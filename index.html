Early Chinese Philosophy

Research

I am interested in debates about metaphysics and action in the early Chinese empire. My dissertation focuses on resonance (感應) as a locus for these debates, with particular attention to Wang Chong (王充).

I am also interested in ancient Greek philosophy and hope to explore our age’s new horizons of global philosophy.

A source I work with

論衡 Lunheng (Discourses Weighed in the Balance)

by Wang Chong (王充, 27–c. 97 CE)
