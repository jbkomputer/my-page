Research

I am interested in debates about metaphysics and action in the early Chinese empire. My dissertation focuses on resonance (感應) as a locus for these debates, with particular attention to Wang Chong (王充).

I am also interested in ancient Greek philosophy and hope to explore our age’s new horizons of global philosophy.

Dissertation: Action at a Distance in Early China
Co-advisors: Mick Hunter and Michael Della Rocca

Teaching

I designed and taught Writing Philosophy: Weakness of Will in Ancient China, Greece, and Today, a writing seminar at Yale (Fall 2024).

Teaching fellow at Yale for Introduction to Ethics, The Chinese Tradition, Introduction to Ancient Philosophy, and Introduction to Ancient Chinese Thought. Instructor of ancient Greek at the City University of New York Latin/Greek Institute (Summer 2025).

Global Philosophy Working Group

In 2022 I founded the Global Philosophy Working Group at Yale, which has since organized 32 events on understudied philosophical traditions: invited talks, dinner workshops, a symposium, an archival session at the Beinecke Library, and three reading series.

Speakers have included Stephen Angle, Jonathan Egid, Henry Jacobs, Hannah Kim, Esther Sunkyung Klein, Hagop Sarkissian, and Bryan Van Norden.
